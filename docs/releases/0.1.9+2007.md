# KalCode 0.1.9 (build 2007)

Source commit: `748427da739076b0156cb258356cb3c05769c9bb`.

## New in this build

- **Tooltips no longer get stuck on screen with reduced motion.** When reduced motion was on, a tooltip that closed right after it opened could stay visible, parked in the top-left corner of the window. Tooltips now show and hide without animation under reduced motion, so they always go away when they close.

## Platforms

- Windows 10 version 1809 or later, x64.
- macOS 14 or later, Apple silicon.
