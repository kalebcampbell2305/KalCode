# Storyboard — KalCode launch film (60.000 s, 1920×1080, 60 fps, 120 BPM)

**Thesis:** software development has too many windows; KalCode turns them into one engineering
cockpit. The film is a product and motion-design film for KalCode. The sample project on
screen is **atlas**, a fictional app.

## Motion standard

The camera is always moving through 3D space. Transitions are designed: zoom-throughs,
whips with real sub-frame motion blur, 3D flips, and fly-in docking. The recurring devices are:

- **Electric energy traces.** A comet of Constellation light runs around the active pane. It turns red on a failure.
- **Beat-landing kinetic type.** Every word arrives exactly on its beat, with anticipation and a streak.
- **Lanes → convergence → ship.** Agent work is light travelling along lanes into `main`, then down one release rail.
- **The picture breathes with the music.** Kicks punch the camera, and claps and impacts light the frame. Both are read from the same `cue_sheet.json` drum blocks as the score, so they land on the exact sample.

## Music

The score is a high-energy electronic track at 120 BPM, moving from E minor to E major. It
uses four-on-the-floor kicks, claps on 2 and 4, 16th hats, a rolling 16th bass with
sidechain pumping, and a supersaw hook built from the KalCode mnemonic (B4 E5 F♯5 B5).
Snare rolls accelerate into the drops at 6, 30 and 48 s, crashes and impacts mark every
scene cut, and the final E major resolution decays to silence at 60.000 s.
